# Roadmap

## Shipped in this revision

- Anonymous browser-local progress; no profiles, streaks, notebook, backups, or live AI generation.
- Generic Journey page; fresh visitors start at zero.
- Seven suggested course paths with visible progression and branches.
- 44 static, transcript-grounded study packs with summaries, terms, mastery questions, reflections, and 682 quiz questions.
- 72 full transcripts, 216 publisher transcript links, and 147 publisher study-guide links.

## Essential content work — still incomplete

The catalog has 1,686 full lectures and 252 supplemental previews. 44 lectures have study packs; 1,642 remain. 72 have inline full transcripts; 216 more link to a publisher transcript. The remaining 1,398 have no verified transcript resource in the app. Publisher study guides are additional external resources, not completed in-app quizzes.

For every remaining lesson: obtain an authorized transcript or transcribe accessible audio, check it against the recording, preserve attribution and permissions, prepare lesson-specific summaries/questions/reflections/readings, and review answer accuracy. Verify assigned readings against actual syllabi; label supplementary readings separately. Add substantive written assignments and rubric-based assessments as courses mature.

YouTube returned a verification challenge during caption retrieval, so caption acquisition could not continue through that route. BiblicalTraining's copyright terms restrict reposting its materials; link to official lectures/transcripts rather than mirroring them. Sources: https://www.biblicaltraining.org/copyright and https://biblicalelearning.org/about/. Linked transcripts have not been silently treated as locally published full text. The previous topic-only Reeves guide was removed from the live library because it was not based on that lecture's transcript.

The machine-readable content-status.json lists coverage for every lesson. Count a lesson complete only after its transcript-grounded pack is actually published.

## MDiv-level curriculum development

The current course collection has not been audited as equivalent to either an undergraduate degree or an MDiv. A collection of lectures alone does not establish graduate-level equivalence.

1. Map learning outcomes and workload against published MDiv curricula and current ATS standards (https://www.ats.edu/Standards-Of-Accreditation). Track depth, prerequisites, reading, and assessed work, not just video hours.
2. Source additional free graduate-level research methods, advanced Hebrew/Greek exegesis, hermeneutics, historical/systematic theology, ethics, and global Christian traditions.
3. Fill practical ministry areas: homiletics, worship, pastoral care, mission, leadership, and religious/community contexts.
4. Add long-form assessed writing, language assessments, research projects, and an integrative capstone with meaningful feedback.
5. Plan supervised field education, spiritual formation, and learning in community. These require people and institutions as well as courseware.
6. Verify access, rights, academic level, and reading costs before adding each course. Gordon-Conwell's free Dimensions of the Faith program is one source to investigate: https://www.gordonconwell.edu/dimensions-of-the-faith/.

The goal is comparable breadth and rigor for independent learning. The app does not award an accredited degree or institutional credit.
